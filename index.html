Research spine — Phonics Glow × AI (EdD)

Phonics Glow (dyslexia-informed twin of Moves) is the EdD research spine. AI-in-education enters via multimodality and multilingual oral/print models across Singapore’s four official languages (English, Mandarin, Malay, Tamil). Pitch/prosody drift in English bot-audio is shaped not only by Singlish but also by mother-tongue transfer — so Standard Singapore English pitch-band QC is a hard fail for page audio, not a soft preference.

Premise: tech-enabled early dyslexia-risk support in all four languages (language-specific orthography + oral models; not English-only decode copied wholesale).

Open question: whether AI-supported multimodal input can help a child sustainably acquire or maintain more than two languages. Beta shelf stays English-first; multilingual Glow is the longer programme. Not a diagnostic/therapy claim; complementary to STELLAR (primary), targeting preschool/early years.

Scarce quadrant

Most strict decodables under-invest in story; most engaging “phonics” books break the code. Flyleaf is the closest Western peer for craft-within-control. Your Moves/Glow bet is that quadrant.

Where strength lives

UK (Dandelion, RWI, Little Wandle) and Australia (LLLL, InitiaLit, SPELD SA, Fitzroy) dominate proper SSP-aligned books. US strength is often sequence (UFLI) + mapped packs, not one literary cast.

Glow gap

Few lines ship a true twin calm edition. Intervention series (Moon Dogs) help older strugglers; your Glow is early-child + design-led, not a separate babyish track.
