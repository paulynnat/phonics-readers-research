Decodable readers — world field dashboard

Pedagogically informed series surveyed for Phonics Moves / Phonics Glow. Not décor phonics. Not STELLAR Big Books. Not CEFR headword readers.

Method: Scores are analytical judgments for Digital Literacy Studio product strategy, not publisher rankings or RCT outcomes. Weighted DLS-fit emphasises baseline strictness, story, dyslexia-informed design, Asia/home — not school-system lock-in.

Scarce quadrant

Most strict decodables under-invest in story; most engaging “phonics” books break the code. Flyleaf is the closest Western peer for craft-within-control. Your Moves/Glow bet is that quadrant.

Where strength lives

UK (Dandelion, RWI, Little Wandle) and Australia (LLLL, InitiaLit, SPELD SA, Fitzroy) dominate proper SSP-aligned books. US strength is often sequence (UFLI) + mapped packs, not one literary cast.

Glow gap

Few lines ship a true twin calm edition. Intervention series (Moon Dogs) help older strugglers; your Glow is early-child + design-led, not a separate babyish track.
